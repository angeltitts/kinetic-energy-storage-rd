# Single-Rotor Commercial Track Roadmap

## Gate 1 — P1 atmospheric energy-recovery bench
Goal: prove closed-loop charge/store/discharge, quantify baseline losses.
Status: manufacturing package defined.

## Gate 2 — P2 low-pressure loss demonstrator
Goal: measure windage reduction versus pressure while keeping the same low-energy rotor.
Output: loss-vs-pressure curve, vacuum hardware burden, seal/pump requirements.

## Gate 3 — P3 bearing-loss reduction
Goal: compare conventional bearings against a non-cryogenic magnetic/hybrid support concept.
Output: coast-down improvement, stiffness/damping, control power.

## Gate 4 — P4 1-5 kW integrated module
Goal: product-like DC-bus module, serviceable enclosure, production-style telemetry.
No energy scaling until containment and bearing model are independently reviewed.

## Gate 5 — P5 application pilot
Choose one:
- crane/hoist peak shaving
- EV fast-charger buffering
- industrial pulse-load smoothing
- battery hybrid life extension

Primary KPI:
$/kW-cycle and avoided infrastructure/battery wear, not Wh/kg.

## Gate 6 — formal FTO + certification path
Before commercial design freeze:
- patent-attorney claim chart
- product safety/certification plan
- containment review
- lifecycle/maintenance model

## Gate 7 — preproduction module
Only after P4/P5 economics and safety gates pass.
