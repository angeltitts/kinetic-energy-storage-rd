# Open Questions

Rank questions by **impact × uncertainty**.

## Q-001 — System energy-density feasibility
Can any DSHC configuration exceed 500 Wh/kg complete-system energy density with 20 GPa / 1.5 g/cc hypothetical CNT material after fatigue, safety factor, containment, stator, cryogenic, and electronics mass?

Priority: CRITICAL

## Q-002 — Progressive containment
What containment architecture can safely arrest rotor failure without requiring containment mass comparable to several times rotor mass?

Priority: CRITICAL

## Q-003 — Rotordynamics
Can a suspended hoop system remain stable at the peripheral speeds implied by 20+ GPa usable fiber stress?

Priority: CRITICAL

## Q-004 — Rotor-zone actuation
What contactless actuation method delivers torque to a small number of independently rotating hoop zones with acceptable rotor heating and stator mass?

Priority: HIGH

## Q-005 — HTS bearing
Can the bearing provide sufficient force, stiffness, damping, and low AC loss while remaining outside the motor-generator ripple field?

Priority: HIGH

## Q-006 — Fatigue and creep
What long-duration stress and cycle-life derating should be applied to candidate CNT/macrofiber systems?

Priority: HIGH
