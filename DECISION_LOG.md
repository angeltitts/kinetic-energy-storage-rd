# Decision Log

## D-001 — Primary storage mechanism
**Decision:** mechanical rotational kinetic storage.

**Reason:** among mechanisms considered, it provides a direct path from improvements in specific tensile strength to higher theoretical specific energy.

## D-002 — Thin hoops instead of thick torus
**Decision:** use thin circumferential hoops / slip layers.

**Reason:** avoid through-thickness and radial matrix stress limiting the longitudinal fiber strength.

## D-003 — No primary SMES
**Decision:** rejected.

**Reason:** magnetic-pressure structural mass dominates system-level energy density.

## D-004 — No plasma storage
**Decision:** rejected.

**Reason:** poor usable mass density, confinement overhead, and no credible long-duration storage advantage.

## D-005 — No full-energy ablation strategy
**Decision:** rejected as primary containment method.

**Reason:** conservation of energy forces impractical thermal loads at multi-kWh/kg storage levels.

## D-006 — System-level metrics
**Decision:** rotor-only Wh/kg is never accepted as the product metric.

**Reason:** containment and parasitic mass may dominate viability.
