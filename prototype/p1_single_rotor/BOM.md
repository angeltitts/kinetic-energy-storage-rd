# P1 Bill of Materials

## Core drive/electronics

1. **Sensored BLDC motor** — Flipsky 5055 200KV 1500W class or equivalent.
2. **VESC-compatible controller** — Mini FSESC 4.20 50A class is sufficient for this low-energy rig when current-limited.
3. **DC energy sink/source** — 6S battery pack or equivalent bidirectional DC source/sink with appropriate BMS and fuse.
4. **Emergency stop** — normally-closed latching mushroom switch that removes motor-controller DC bus power.
5. **Main fuse** — sized below wiring/controller safe current for the commissioning limit.
6. **RPM sensor** — independent Hall/optical tachometer channel.
7. **Accelerometer** — frame-mounted 3-axis sensor.
8. **DC power meter/logger** — voltage/current logging on DC bus.

## Mechanical

1. 6061-T6 aluminum rotor disk, 200 mm x 6 mm.
2. 12 mm precision steel shaft.
3. 2 x KP001-class 12 mm pillow-block bearings.
4. Custom split-clamp rotor hub for 12 mm shaft.
5. Flexible shaft coupling or 1:1 HTD belt drive matching selected motor shaft.
6. 400 x 300 x 12 mm minimum rigid base plate.
7. 6 mm clear polycarbonate rotor guard.
8. Fasteners, threadlocker, shaft collars, cable clamps.

## Current online reference prices

- Flipsky 5055 200KV sensored BLDC motor: currently advertised from about $43.
- Flipsky Mini FSESC 4.20: currently advertised about $56.
- KP001 12 mm pillow-block bearing: commodity units are available around $5-13 each depending supplier.

These are procurement references, not locked vendors. Final purchase should confirm shaft size, sensor connector, controller voltage range, and current availability.
