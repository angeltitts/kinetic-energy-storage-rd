# P1 Hub and Rotor Machining Drawing

Units: mm

This is a deliberately conservative low-energy prototype interface. It is not a high-speed production flywheel hub.

## Rotor disk

Material:
- 6061-T6 aluminum plate

Dimensions:
- OD: 200.00 +/-0.10
- thickness: 6.00 +/-0.10
- center bore: 12.50 +0.10/-0.00
- 6 x through holes: diameter 5.5
- hole-circle diameter: 44.0
- angular spacing: 60 degrees
- all holes equally spaced and concentric with rotor OD

Finish:
- deburr both faces
- break sharp edges 0.3 max
- do not countersink rotor mounting holes
- machine OD and hole pattern in one setup where possible

Balance preparation:
- do not add arbitrary balancing holes before first static balance check
- record finished mass
- mark one angular datum on rotor rim

## Clamp hub pair

Use two identical flange pieces, one on each side of the rotor.

Material:
- 6061-T6 aluminum

Each flange:
- OD: 60.0
- overall thickness: 12.0
- central shaft bore: 12.00 H7 preferred
- flange pilot shoulder: 24.0 OD x 1.5 long
- pilot recess on rotor side may be omitted if both flange faces are flat and concentric
- 6 x M5 holes on 44.0 PCD, 60 degree spacing

Front flange:
- 6 x M5 clearance holes: 5.5
- one radial clamp split, width 1.5-2.0
- one transverse M5 pinch-bolt feature across split

Rear flange:
- 6 x M5 x 0.8 tapped holes on 44.0 PCD
- one radial clamp split, width 1.5-2.0
- one transverse M5 pinch-bolt feature across split

Assembly bolts:
- 6 x M5 class 8.8 socket-head screws
- thread into rear flange
- use medium removable threadlocker
- tighten in star pattern
- final torque per screw manufacturer for dry/treated M5 threads; do not exceed the rear-flange thread capacity

Shaft clamping:
- both flanges independently clamp the 12 mm shaft
- clamp faces must remain parallel to rotor
- apply witness paint across each flange/shaft interface after assembly

## Axial arrangement

rear hub flange | rotor disk 6 mm | front hub flange

The disk is captured between two machined aluminum faces; drive torque is transferred by the six axial bolts and flange friction, while both hub halves clamp the shaft.

## Inspection

After assembly:
- rotor radial runout at OD <=0.25
- rotor axial wobble at OD <=0.25
- no visible shaft/hub slip under hand torque
- no bolt heads or hub features interfere with bearing or guard
- witness marks applied before powered testing

If tolerances cannot be met, do not compensate by increasing speed.
