# P1 Motor Adjustment Plate

Purpose:
hold the Flipsky 5055 motor and tension the 400 mm HTD 5M belt.

## Geometry

Plate:
- 6061-T6 or mild steel
- 120 x 90 x 6 mm minimum

Base slots:
- 4 x slots, 7 mm wide x 22 mm long
- slot direction parallel to pulley centerline
- provide at least +/-10 mm motor center-distance adjustment around nominal 118.3 mm

Motor holes:
- do not pre-drill from this document unless the exact Flipsky motor mounting-face drawing supplied with the purchased unit is verified
- transfer the purchased motor mounting pattern to the plate or update CAD from the vendor drawing before machining

## Alignment

The motor pulley and shaft pulley must:
- have coplanar tooth faces within 0.5 mm
- show no visible belt tracking against either flange
- allow finger-deflection tensioning without excessive bearing side load

## Belt

HTD 5M:
- 15 mm width
- 400 mm effective length
- 80 teeth

Do not over-tension. P1 torque is intentionally low.
