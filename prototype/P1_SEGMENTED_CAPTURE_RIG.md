# P1 Prototype Concept — Segmented Isolation and Capture Rig

## Objective

Test the Phase 3 anti-cascade hypothesis at deliberately low stored energy.

P1 is **not** a high-speed flywheel prototype and must not attempt multi-km/s operation or destructive full-energy containment.

## Hypothesis

A rotor stack made of many mechanically independent hoop elements can fail locally without forcing neighboring hoops into a common-mode failure, provided the stack includes sufficient radial/axial isolation and a local catcher path.

## Test article

Use a small guarded stack containing several lightweight hoop elements.

The experiment should permit one selected hoop element to be brought to a controlled stop or released from its normal support condition while neighboring elements remain in benign, low-energy rotation.

Do not use brittle or high-energy rotors for this test.

## Measurements

- speed of every hoop before, during, and after the local event
- vibration of the shared frame
- displacement/contact indication
- temperature near catcher surfaces
- neighboring-hoop speed disturbance
- whether any secondary contact occurs

## Pass criteria

P1 passes if a local event:

- remains confined to the selected hoop;
- does not produce sustained contact with adjacent hoops;
- does not force shutdown through an uncontrolled cascade;
- can be detected by instrumentation;
- can be repeated with consistent behavior at low energy.

## Fail criteria

P1 falsifies the current segmented-containment direction if local hoop contact reliably triggers neighboring hoop instability even at low stored energy, or if the isolation/catcher geometry becomes so massive or complex that scaling toward the Phase 2 mass budget is implausible.

## Program sequence

1. P0 — differential independent hoop rotation and sensing
2. P1 — local anti-cascade isolation/capture
3. P2 — contactless zone drive at low energy
4. P3 — magnetic suspension / vacuum loss characterization at low energy
5. only after independent engineering review: progressively higher-energy rotor work in a qualified remote facility

## Safety rule

Any physical build based on this document must be independently reviewed by a qualified mechanical engineer. The autonomous R&D agent is limited to low-energy concept validation and does not authorize high-energy rotating tests.
