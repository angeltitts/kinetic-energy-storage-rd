# Phase 1 Bench Prototype

The CAD generator produces a low-energy geometry demonstrator containing a 25 mm shaft bore, 75 mm hub, three concentric 2 mm-wall hoops separated by 0.5 mm radial gaps, an oblate housing shell, and a central cryostat pocket.

The geometry is for dimensional and integration checks only. It is not a certified pressure vessel, cryostat, flywheel, or burst-containment design.
