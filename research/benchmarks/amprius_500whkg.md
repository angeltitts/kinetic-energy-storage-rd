# Competitive Benchmark — Amprius 500 Wh/kg Silicon-Anode Cell

Source: Amprius Technologies announcement, March 23, 2023

URL: https://amprius.com/the-all-new-amprius-500-wh-kg-battery-platform-is-here/

## Reported / source-supported facts

- Amprius announced a silicon-anode lithium-ion cell platform exceeding 500 Wh/kg.
- The company reported independent verification by Mobile Power Solutions.
- Reported test result: >504 Wh/kg and >1321 Wh/L at 25 °C.
- The platform was positioned for applications where low mass and long endurance are critical, including aviation.
- Amprius stated that prototypes would ship to selected customers during 2023.
- The same announcement stated that its commercially available cells at that time delivered up to 450 Wh/kg and 1150 Wh/L.

## Why this matters to DSHC

The DSHC Phase 0 target of 500 Wh/kg complete-system energy density is not an abstract aspirational number. It now sits directly against a demonstrated lithium-ion cell-level benchmark.

This changes the engineering question from:

> Can DSHC reach 500 Wh/kg?

into:

> Can DSHC reach or exceed 500 Wh/kg at the complete-system/module level while providing a compelling advantage in cycle life, peak power, recharge rate, self-discharge, safety, service life, or cost?

A kinetic architecture that only matches ~500 Wh/kg while requiring substantially more containment, cryogenic support, difficult manufacturing, or unacceptable failure energy may have no practical advantage over advanced electrochemistry.

## Benchmarking implications

Future feasibility reports should compare DSHC against at least these metrics:

- complete-system gravimetric energy density (Wh/kg)
- volumetric energy density (Wh/L)
- cycle life at useful depth of discharge
- round-trip efficiency
- standby/self-discharge loss
- peak and continuous power density
- recharge time / allowable charge power
- operating temperature range
- safety and failure containment burden
- manufacturing maturity / TRL
- estimated cost per kWh

## Evidence classification

- Source type: manufacturer announcement
- Energy-density measurement: stated as independently verified by Mobile Power Solutions
- Commercial-maturity statements: manufacturer-reported; distinguish prototype platform from cells described as commercially available in the same announcement
- Date sensitivity: 2023 benchmark; later Amprius products may exceed it and should be tracked separately
